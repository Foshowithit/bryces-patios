# Byces Patios — social media launch kit

The website is the anchor. Social is the top of the funnel: it's where a neighbour sees a
before/after, taps the profile, and ends up on the estimate form.

**The whole strategy in one line:** post the before/after. Everything else supports it.

---

## 1. Claim the handles first

Grab these **today**, before someone else does. Same handle everywhere so it's easy to say
out loud and easy to search.

| Platform | Handle | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | `@bycespatios` | Primary. Where homeowners under 50 look. |
| Facebook | `bycespatios` | Still the strongest platform for 45+ homeowners — and the biggest local-referral driver. Do not skip it. |

## 3. Content pillars

Rotate through these. Every post belongs to one of them.

| Pillar | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Before / After** | The slider shots, as a carousel or a video | The single highest-performing format in this trade. People cannot scroll past it. |
| **Process** | Base prep, gravel, string lines, cutting stone | Proves competence. Most competitors only show the pretty finish, so showing the boring part is a differentiator. |
| **Finished work** | Beauty shots, golden hour, lights on | Aspiration. This is what people save and send to their partner. |
| **Education** | "Why we dig 8 inches", material comparisons, drainage | Positions Byce as the expert, not just a pair of hands. Also very shareable. |
| **Social proof** | Reviews, thank-you notes, repeat customers | Converts the people who are already interested. |
| **Local** | Attleboro, the truck, the crew, New England seasons | Makes the business feel like a neighbour, not a franchise. |

**Rule of thumb: for every 1 "look how nice this is" post, do 2 "here's what it took" posts.**

## 4. First 30 days

One post per weekday, plus one story per day. Cross-post Facebook and Instagram. Re-cut the
video posts for TikTok and Shorts.

### Week 1 — Introduce and prove

| Day | Pillar | Post |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Local | **Launch post.** "We started Byces Patios. Here's the first patio we finished." Best before/after photo you have. |
| 2 | Process | Carousel: 4 photos of a build in progress, base to finished stone. |
| 3 | Education | "Why a patio fails: it's almost never the stone." Talk about the base. |
| 4 | Finished | Golden-hour shot. One sentence. Let the photo work. |
| 5 | Social proof | Screenshot of the first review or thank-you text (blur the name). |

### Week 2 — Before/After week

| Day | Pillar | Post |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Before/After | Reel: before → after with a wipe transition. Best-performing format there is. |
| 7 | Process | Close-up of a cut edge. "This is the detail nobody notices. It's the one we care about most." |
| 8 | Education | Material comparison: sandstone vs bluestone vs pavers. Which one for which yard. |
| 9 | Before/After | Second pair. Carousel, before first, after last. |
| 10 | Local | Byce on site, truck, tools, early morning. Human face. |

### Week 3 — Establish expertise

| Day | Pillar | Post |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | Education | "Three questions to ask any patio contractor." |
| 12 | Process | Drainage and grading — how water is kept away from the house. |
| 13 | Finished | Fire pit at dusk, lights on. |
| 14 | Social proof | Another review. |
| 15 | Education | "What $X actually buys you in a patio." Cost transparency performs extremely well. |

### Week 4 — Convert

| Day | Pillar | Post |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | Before/After | Third pair. |
| 17 | Process | Time-lapse of a full day (even a phone propped on a bucket works). |
| 18 | Education | "It's [month]. If you want a patio this season, here's when to book." |
| 19 | Finished | Wide shot, whole yard. |
| 20 | Local + CTA | "Booking [next month]. Free estimates — link in bio." |

Then repeat the cycle. By week 5 there's enough of a library that a week of content takes
twenty minutes to assemble.

## 5. Caption formulas

Short beats clever. The photo does the work; the caption just needs to not get in the way.

**Before/After**
> Before → after.
> Four days, one crew.
> Attleboro, MA. Free estimates — link in bio.

**Process**
> This is the part nobody photographs.
> 8 inches of compacted base. It's why the surface will still be flat in ten years.
> Norton, MA.

**Education**
> Three questions to ask before you hire anyone to build a patio:
> 1. How deep do you dig?
> 2. How do you handle drainage?
> 3. Can I see a job from three years ago?
> If they can't answer all three, keep looking.

**Finished**
> Sandstone, cedar, and about a thousand cups of coffee.
> Rehoboth, MA.

**Local / CTA**
> We build within about 40 minutes of Attleboro.
> Booking for [month]. Free on-site estimates — link in bio.

## 7. Practical notes

**Posting times.** Weekdays 6–8am or 5–7pm (people on the way to and from work), and Sunday
evenings. Test and see what your audience does.

**Video beats photos.** Every before/after should exist as both. A 12-second wipe transition
will outperform the same image every single time.

**Shoot as you work.** The biggest mistake new contractors make is not documenting. A phone
in a pocket, 20 seconds per stage: excavation, base, first stone, finished. That's four posts
from one job and it costs nothing.

**Landscape, but crop-safe.** Shoot 4:5 vertical for the feed and keep the subject centred so
the same file works as a 9:16 Reel.

**Batch it.** Photograph every job properly, edit once a week, schedule a week ahead. Do not
try to post daily from scratch.

**Reply to every comment and DM.** An unanswered "how much for a patio?" is a lost job. Have
a stock reply ready:

> Thanks for reaching out! Pricing depends on size, material and access, so we do a free
> on-site estimate rather than guessing. Want to send me your town and roughly what you're
> thinking? — [name]

**Ask for the review while you're still on site.** That's when goodwill is highest. Send
them the direct Google review link by text before you leave. This is worth more than any
post.

**Don't buy followers, don't buy ads yet.** For the first six months, organic local reach and
Google Business will do more than a paid campaign, and they're free.
