# Bryce's Patios — Citation / Directory Package

**Purpose:** identical NAP + listing text across every directory, so Google's local
index can reconcile one business instead of many half-signals. Inconsistency between
listings actively suppresses local ranking; consistency across them is a ranking input.

**Status legend:** `FREE` = free listing, `FREE+PAID` = free tier exists, paid upgrade optional,
`PAID` = no meaningful free listing.

> **CRITICAL — name field:** the business name is `Bryce's Patios` on every listing.
> Never `Bryce's Patios | Patio Installer Mansfield MA`, never `Bryce's Patios - Stone
> Paving & Hardscaping`. Keyword-stuffed names are the #1 cause of GBP suspension and
> every major directory rejects or de-ranks them too.

> **CRITICAL — address visibility:** Bryce is a **service-area business (SAB)**. On
> Google (and Nextdoor) **hide the street address** and use the service-area list.
> On Yelp/Angi/Houzz/BBB/Facebook the directory generally *wants* a business address —
> put `885 West St, Mansfield, MA 02048` and do **not** tick "show my address publicly"
> where a "hide address / customers come to me" toggle exists. Consistency of the
> **address string** matters; public visibility of it does not.

**12-town service area (Massachusetts only)** — use this exact list on every directory that
accepts free text. It matches the site and Google (`SEO/GBP.md` §4):

Massachusetts — Mansfield, Attleboro, North Attleboro, Norton, Foxborough, Seekonk,
Rehoboth, Plainville, Franklin, Taunton, Easton, Sharon.

> **No Rhode Island anywhere.** RI ch. 5-65 requires a registered RI contractor number in
> any advertising that names RI work, and hardscaping is covered work. Until Bryce is
> registered in RI and we have that number, do **not** list RI towns, write "MA & RI", or
> claim Rhode Island coverage on any listing. If he registers later, add RI here, on the
> site, and on Google together.
