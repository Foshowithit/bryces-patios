# Bryce's Patios — Citation / Directory Package

**Purpose:** identical NAP + listing text across every directory, so Google's local
index can reconcile one business instead of many half-signals. Inconsistency between
listings actively suppresses local ranking; consistency across them is a ranking input.

**Status legend:** `FREE` = free listing, `FREE+PAID` = free tier exists, paid upgrade optional,
`PAID` = no meaningful free listing.

> put `885 West St, Mansfield, MA 02048` and do **not** tick "show my address publicly"

## 2. Per-platform listing sheet

### Google Business Profile — `FREE`
- Claim/create: **https://business.google.com/us/business-profile/** (verified 200)
- Category: `Paving Contractor` (primary), `Masonry Contractor` + `Landscaper` (secondary)
- Full buildout, verification video script, Q&A, posts, photos: **see `SEO/GBP.md`**.
- Nothing else on this page duplicates GBP — that file is the source of truth.

### Yelp for Business — `FREE+PAID`
- Claim an existing page / add a business: **https://business.yelp.com/claim**
  (redirects to https://business.yelp.com/campaign/add/ — verified 200)
- Business owners page: **https://business.yelp.com/** (verified 200)
- **Free tier:** claim page, add hours, photos, categories, respond to reviews. Do this.
- **Paid tier (Yelp Ads):** skip. No budget, and paid Yelp does not help organic local ranking.
- Name: `Bryce's Patios`. Primary category: `Paving Contractors` → secondary
  `Landscaping`, `Masonry/Concrete`.
- **Do not** pay Yelp to hide competitors' ads off your page. Ignore their calls.

### Angi (formerly Angie's List) — `FREE+PAID`
- Pro signup: **https://signup.angi.com/pro** (verified 200)
- **Free tier:** a free business profile with photos, description, and reviews.
- **Paid tier:** Angi Leads is pay-per-lead and aggressively upsold — **do not enroll
  until Bryce has capacity for high-volume shared leads.** A one-man shop paying for
  shared leads loses money. Free profile only.
- Category: `Patios` / `Paving Contractors` / `Landscape Contractors`.

### Houzz Pro — `FREE+PAID`
- Pro signup: **https://pro.houzz.com/pro** (verified 200)
- **Free tier:** free professional profile, project photos, description, reviews.
  Houzz is design-led, so the strongest patio photos get the most profile views.
- **Paid tier (Houzz Pro):** skip for now. Free profile captures the SEO value.
- Categories: `Patio Design`, `Landscape Contractors`, `Paving Contractors`.
- Upload the same 12 photos used for GBP, with the same captions.

### Nextdoor Business — `FREE`
- Business page: **https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/small-business** (verified 200)
- **Free.** Strong for hyperlocal neighborhood visibility in the exact towns Bryce
  serves, and Nextdoor Business pages can surface in Google. Set the service area to
  the same 22 towns plus nearby.
- Category: `Landscaping` / `Contractor` / `Home Improvement`.

### Better Business Bureau — `FREE (unaccredited) / PAID (accredited)`
- Get accredited / create a listing: **https://www.bbb.org/get-accredited** (verified 200)
- Search for a profile: **https://www.bbb.org/**
- **Free tier:** BBB maintains a free business profile (name, address, phone, website,
  basic info) and accepts customer complaints/reviews. Create/claim the free profile.
  You do **not** have to be accredited to be listed.
- **Paid tier (accreditation):** costs annually, no budget, and accreditation is a trust
  badge, not a ranking driver. Skip until revenue justifies it.
- **BBB rating caveat:** with no reviews yet, the profile shows "Not Rated" / no rating.
  That is honest and fine. Never solicit fake reviews to fill it.

### Facebook Page — `FREE`
- Create a Page: **https://www.facebook.com/pages/create** (verified reachable)
- Business/Pages hub: **https://www.facebook.com/business**
- **Free.** Category `Paving & Asphalt Service` / `Landscaping` / `Contractor`. Add the
  website, phone, hours, service area, and the same photos. The Page is also the
  destination for the `sameAs` social URLs already in the site schema — **claim the
  handle `@brycespatios` here first** since the footer already links it.
- Business Manager / Ads: **https://adsmanager.facebook.com/** — skip; no paid ads.

### Google Search Console — `FREE` (required for item 5 monitoring)
- **https://search.google.com/search-console** — add `brycespatios.work` as a Domain
  property, verify via the DNS TXT record Porkbun exposes. Then submit the sitemap
  (`https://brycespatios.work/sitemap.xml`) under Sitemaps. This is what turns
  "invisible in search" into measurable impressions/clicks over time.
