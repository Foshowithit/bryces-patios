01 — The promise

Make more of the space you call home.

Most backyards are one good decision away from becoming the best room in the house. We build patios, walkways and outdoor living spaces laid out around how you actually live — not around a catalogue.

Every job starts with a walk around your yard and an honest conversation about slope, sun, drainage and budget. Then we build it properly: real excavation, a compacted base, the right restraint on every edge, and stone set to last through New England winters.

  • i

    Built to stay put

    Proper depth, compacted base, locked edges. The part you can't see is the part that keeps the part you can see straight.

  • ii

    Designed for your yard

    No copy-paste layouts. The shape follows your slope, your doorways and the way you want to use the space.

  • iii

    One crew, start to finish

    The person who quotes the job is the person on site until the last joint is swept. No handing you off.

03 — The transformation

See what a backyard can become.

Drag the handle. Same yard, four days apart.

  1. Day 1

    Layout & excavation

    Set the shape and elevations, strip the sod, dig to full depth.

  2. Day 2

    Base & compaction

    Crushed stone in lifts, compacted and screeded dead flat.

  3. Day 3

    Setting the stone

    Stone laid to pattern, cut to fit, joints swept and filled.

  4. Day 4

    Details & clean-up

    Edge restraint, finishing touches, and a site left cleaner than we found it.

04 — Craftsmanship

The details make the difference.

  • Cut, don't force

    Every stone that meets an edge, a step or a wall gets cut to fit. No gaps filled with sand and hope.

  • Drainage first

    Water goes where we want it to go. Slope, base and joints are all part of the same plan.

  • Built for the freeze

    Massachusetts winters are the real inspector. Depth and compaction are how we pass.

05 — About Bryce's

Get to know the people behind the work.

Bryce's Patios is a local, owner-operated hardscaping business. It started the way most good trades do — someone who cared more about getting the detail right than about how fast the job could be finished, and neighbours who noticed.

We keep the crew small on purpose. It means one point of contact, one standard, and Bryce answering the phone three years after the job is done. We work within a short drive of Attleboro, so we're never far from a project that needs a look.

What you can expect

  • A free on-site visit and a written estimate before anything starts
  • A clear scope — what's included, what isn't, and what it costs
  • One crew on site, start to finish
  • A tidy site every evening and a clean one at the end
  • A straight answer if we think you don't need the work
